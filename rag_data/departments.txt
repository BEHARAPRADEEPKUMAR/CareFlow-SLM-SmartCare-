CareFlow Hospital Departments

Cardiology:
Provides consultation services related to heart and cardiovascular health.

Dermatology:
Provides consultation services related to skin, hair, and nail concerns.

Orthopedics:
Provides consultation services related to bones, joints, muscles, and the musculoskeletal system.

General Medicine:
Provides general medical consultation and routine healthcare services.

Pediatrics:
Provides healthcare services for infants, children, and adolescents.

ENT:
Provides consultation services related to the ear, nose, and throat.
