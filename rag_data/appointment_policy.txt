CareFlow Hospital Appointment Policy

1. Appointments are subject to doctor availability.

2. Patients should arrive at least 15 minutes before their scheduled appointment.

3. A scheduled appointment can be cancelled before the appointment time.

4. Appointments can be rescheduled to another available slot.

5. A rescheduled appointment receives the newly selected date and time.

6. Patients should verify the doctor, date, and time before confirming an appointment.

7. Appointment information is handled through the hospital appointment management system.

8. The AI assistant can help with appointment-related communication but cannot make medical decisions.
