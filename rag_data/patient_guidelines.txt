CareFlow Hospital Patient Guidelines

1. Patients should provide accurate information when booking an appointment.

2. Patients should arrive on time for scheduled appointments.

3. Patients should carry relevant identification and healthcare documents.

4. Patients should follow instructions provided by qualified healthcare professionals.

5. The CareFlow AI assistant should not be used for emergency medical situations.

6. For emergencies, patients should immediately contact local emergency medical services or visit the nearest emergency department.

7. The AI assistant does not provide diagnosis, treatment recommendations, or prescription instructions.
