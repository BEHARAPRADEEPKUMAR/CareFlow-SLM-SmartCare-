# CAREFLOW HOSPITAL - PATIENT INFORMATION

## ABOUT CAREFLOW HOSPITAL

CareFlow Hospital provides outpatient healthcare services through multiple medical departments. Patients can use CareFlow to find doctors, book appointments, manage appointments, and communicate with the hospital.

## HOSPITAL SERVICES

CareFlow Hospital provides the following services:

1. Cardiology
2. Dermatology
3. Orthopedics
4. General Medicine
5. Appointment scheduling
6. Appointment rescheduling
7. Appointment cancellation
8. Patient communication
9. Appointment reminders

## DOCTOR SPECIALIZATIONS

### CARDIOLOGY

The Cardiology department handles heart and cardiovascular-related consultations.

### DERMATOLOGY

The Dermatology department handles skin, hair, and related dermatological consultations.

### ORTHOPEDICS

The Orthopedics department handles bone, joint, muscle, and musculoskeletal consultations.

### GENERAL MEDICINE

The General Medicine department provides general medical consultations and primary healthcare services.

## APPOINTMENT BOOKING

Patients can book an appointment through the CareFlow AI assistant. The assistant can identify the requested department, doctor, date, and time and then search available appointment slots.

Patients must be authenticated before an appointment can be created.

## APPOINTMENT RESCHEDULING

Patients can reschedule an existing appointment when a suitable new appointment slot is available.

The system checks whether the requested doctor already has an appointment at the requested date and time.

## APPOINTMENT CANCELLATION

Patients can cancel their appointments through the CareFlow appointment management system.

Cancelled appointments are retained in the system for record purposes and are not permanently deleted.

## DOCTOR APPOINTMENTS

Doctors can view appointments assigned to them.

Doctors can confirm scheduled appointments, mark completed appointments, or cancel appointments.

## EMERGENCY SAFETY

CareFlow AI is designed for appointment and patient communication workflows. It does not replace emergency medical services.

Patients experiencing a medical emergency should contact their local emergency service or visit the nearest emergency department.

## MEDICAL ADVICE

CareFlow AI should not independently diagnose medical conditions or prescribe medication.

For medical diagnosis, treatment decisions, or medication advice, patients should consult a qualified healthcare professional.

## PATIENT COMMUNICATION

Patients can use the CareFlow AI assistant for appointment-related questions and hospital information.

The AI assistant can provide information from the hospital knowledge base using retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).

## APPOINTMENT REMINDERS

CareFlow can provide appointment reminder functionality for upcoming appointments.

## PATIENT DATA

CareFlow should use authenticated access when handling patient-specific appointment information.

Patient information should not be exposed to unauthorized users.
